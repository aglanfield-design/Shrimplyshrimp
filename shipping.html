🛡️ 1. 100% Live Arrival Guarantee (DOA Policy)

We take every precaution to ensure your shrimp arrive healthy and active. However, live animal shipping carries inherent risks. If the unthinkable happens and your shrimp do not survive the journey, we have you covered.

Important: Do not open the shipping bag or discard deceased livestock until clear photos are taken and your claim is submitted!

How to Claim Your Guarantee:

  1. Receive the Package: You must accept delivery on the first attempt, or pick up the box within 2 hours if held for pickup at your local post office.
  2. Take Clear Photos: Within 2 hours of confirmed delivery, take 2–3 clear photos and a brief video of the deceased shrimp inside the unopened shipping bag next to the shipping label.
  3. Email Our Team: Send your photos and order number to theshrimplyshrimp@gmail.com with the subject line "DOA Claim - Order #".
  4. Resolution: Verified DOA claims receive store credit or replacement shrimp. Note: Original shipping charges are non-refundable.

📦 2. Shipping Schedule & Weather Safety

Live creatures shouldn't spend weekends sitting in dark, unconditioned shipping warehouses. To protect your livestock, we follow strict dispatch schedules and weather monitoring.

When We Ship

We dispatch live livestock orders exclusively on Mondays and Tuesdays (and Wednesdays for Express orders). Orders placed Thursday through Sunday ship the following Monday.

Weather Cutoffs & Holds

If temperatures anywhere along the transit route drop below 35°F (2°C) or exceed 90°F (32°C), we will hold shipment until weather stabilizes. Complimentary heat or ice packs are added automatically when needed!

❄️ 3. Professional Packaging Standards

Every shipment is packed using specialized aquarium livestock insulation designed to minimize shock and maintain water stability throughout transit.

  • ✔
    Styrofoam Thermal Insulated Boxes: High-density foam inserts buffer external temperature spikes and drops.
  • ✔

  • ✔
    Automatic Heat & Cold Packs: 72-hour heat packs or phase 22 ice packs are included free of charge depending on seasonal climate.

📍 5. Delivery Restrictions & Drip Acclimation

Geographic Coverage

We currently ship livestock only within the Continental United States. Due to strict agricultural and state wildlife laws, we cannot ship live livestock to Hawaii, Alaska, US Territories, or internationally.

Post-Arrival Acclimation

Shrimp are sensitive to sudden changes in pH, GH, and TDS. You must drip acclimate your shrimp for 1.5 to 2 hours before releasing them into your tank.

🔬 Quarantine & Breeding Transparency

We know how devastating it is to introduce pests or disease into an established, thriving shrimp colony. That is why we practice absolute transparency about where our livestock comes from.

  • ✔
    Home-Bred Lines: Our signature lines are bred in-house in strict, predator-free environments.
  • ✔
    Imported Stock Quarantine: Any imported varieties undergo a mandatory 30-day quarantine before they are ever listed on our website.
  • ✔
    100% Pest-Free: We actively inspect and treat for common hobby pests, including Scutariella japonica, Planaria, Vorticella, and Hydra. You receive only clean, healthy, active shrimp.
