Water Parameters

To ensure your shrimp thrive, we recommend matching our breeding parameters closely:

pH 5.5–6.2
Potential of Hydrogen: Measures water acidity. Caridina shrimp need acidic water to thrive and breed safely.
GH 4–6
General Hardness: Measures calcium and magnesium. These minerals are essential for your shrimp to build strong shells and molt successfully.
KH 0–1
Carbonate Hardness: Measures buffering capacity. This is kept at 0-1 so the active soil can easily lower the pH without resistance.
TDS 110–140
Total Dissolved Solids: The combined total of all minerals and organics in the water. Used as a gauge to ensure water isn't too "thick".
Temp 68–72°F
Temperature: Cooler water holds more oxygen and reduces bacterial infections. 68-72°F is the sweet spot for health and breeding.
