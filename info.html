Our Mission

At Shrimply Shrimp, we specialize in breeding high-quality freshwater shrimp with a focus on health, color, and consistency. Every shrimp is carefully raised in stable, well-maintained tanks to ensure strong genetics and vibrant appearance.

We are passionate about the shrimp hobby and committed to providing hobbyists and collectors with high grade good looking shrimp. Whether you're just starting out or expanding your collection, our goal is to deliver shrimp that thrive in your aquarium.

Our Process

Each shrimp is bred and raised in controlled environments with consistent water parameters, proper nutrition, and regular monitoring. We take the time to ensure quality over quantity.

We use proven packing methods to keep shrimp safe during transit. Orders are shipped with insulation and care to maintain temperature and water quality. Our mission is to provide reliable, high-quality shrimp while helping grow the shrimp-keeping community through transparency and care.

We strongly advise against using tap water. Its parameters can fluctuate significantly and it often contains minerals that raise KH levels. We highly recommend using RO/DI or purified water, remineralized with a dedicated GH+ product such as SaltyShrimp Bee Shrimp Mineral GH+.

We highly recommend using the drip acclimation method for at least 1.5 to 2 hours. Shrimp are very sensitive to sudden changes in water parameters, temperature, and pH, so slow acclimation is essential for their survival.

Most Neocaridina (like Cherry Shrimp) thrive in temperatures between 68°F and 78°F (20°C–25°C). Caridina (like Black BOAs) species generally prefer slightly cooler water, ideally between 65°F and 72°F (18°C–22°C).
